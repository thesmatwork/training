# 08. Learning and Farmer Community

## Learning
- Guides, short lessons, and videos on crop selection, soil, irrigation, pests, organic methods, and modern farming
- Filter by crop, topic, language, and skill level
- Mark lessons as saved/completed where supported
- Use approved/licensed video links or user-owned media

## Community
- Farmer posts and crop photos
- Comments, likes/helpful votes, and topic/crop groups
- English, Telugu, and Hindi readiness
- Report post/comment and moderation workflow
- Avoid exposing personal contact details or private farm data by default
