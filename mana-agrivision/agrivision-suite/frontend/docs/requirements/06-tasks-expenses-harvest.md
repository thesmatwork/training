# 06. Tasks, Expenses, Harvest and Profit

## Tasks
- Watering, sowing, fertilizing/composting, pest monitoring, pruning, and harvest tasks
- Due date, priority, status, notes, and recurring schedule
- In-app notifications; email/mobile alerts only after provider setup
- Task history and weather-aware suggestions

## Expenses and inventory
- Record amount, category, date, farm/crop, vendor, notes, and receipt photo when supported
- Track seeds, fertilizer, compost, pesticides, tools, and stock quantity
- Warn about low stock where inventory data exists

## Harvest and profitability
- Record quantity, unit, date, crop, quality, sale price, buyer/market, and notes
- Compare expected vs actual yield
- Calculate revenue, expenses, and net profit from recorded data
- Export reports to PDF/Excel where implemented
