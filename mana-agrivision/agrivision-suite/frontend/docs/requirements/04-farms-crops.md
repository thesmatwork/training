# 04. Farm and Crop Management

## Farm fields
- Farm name and location
- Land area and unit (acre, hectare, etc.)
- Soil type/soil health notes
- Irrigation method and water source
- Farm photos and notes

## Crop fields
- Crop name and variety
- Farm association
- Season and sowing date
- Expected harvest date and actual harvest date
- Crop stage and notes
- Crop photos and previous crop history
- Expected yield and actual yield

## Actions
- Create, view, edit, and delete owned farms/crops
- Filter crops by farm, season, and status
- Validate required fields and units
- Keep ownership checks on the backend
