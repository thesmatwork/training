# 09. Government Schemes, Marketplace and Reports

## Government schemes
- Central and state schemes
- Eligibility, required documents, benefits, official links, deadlines, and last-verified date
- Search and filters by state, crop, category, and eligibility
- Show source links and do not invent deadlines or eligibility rules

## Marketplace
- Browse farming supplies/services and product details
- Search/filter and contact/order flow only when a real backend is configured
- Clearly identify demo listings if no live marketplace integration exists
- Do not collect payment details without a secure payment provider and compliance review

## Reports
- Farm, season, crop, expense, harvest, yield, and profitability summaries
- Date/farm/crop filters
- PDF/Excel export where implemented
- Reports must be generated from the user's authorized records only
