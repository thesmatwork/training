# 05. Weather and Market Information

## Weather
- Location-based current weather and forecast
- Temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind, and forecast periods when provider data supports them
- Weather-aware reminders for farm tasks
- Show last-updated time and data-source errors

## Market prices
- Select crop and market/mandi
- Display price, unit, market name, and date of update
- Save/remove watchlist items
- Show a clear unavailable/stale-data state when an official data source is unavailable

## Integration notes
Weather and mandi prices require API/provider configuration. Do not present sample/demo values as live values.
